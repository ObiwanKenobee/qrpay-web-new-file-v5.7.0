# M029-A — Atlas Care Pilot Qualification Pack

**Status:** Working template — no pilot selected or approved

> **Important:** This pack is for evaluating a prospective operating partner and a bounded service workflow. It is not a claim that Atlas Care has a pilot, partner, participant, safeguarding capacity or regulatory approval. Do not enter live participant information, health/welfare details, identity documents, phone numbers, exact household locations or confidential case narratives into this document or the repository.

## 1. Qualification objective

Identify one real operating organization and one bounded, non-cash service workflow that could be responsibly supported by Atlas Care.

The platform's proposed role is coordination and accountable record-keeping. It does not itself promise to deliver a partner's service, determine legal entitlement, provide emergency response, offer clinical care, conduct statutory case management or move money.

The purpose of qualification is to establish whether there is a real service problem, an accountable operator with the people and authority to address it, a proportionate data model, and an outcome that can be measured without compromising dignity or safety.

## 2. Evidence discipline

For every material statement, assign one classification:

| Classification | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Verified fact | Supported by a current, attributable source | Organization registration checked against the issuing authority |
| Partner declaration | Statement made by an authorized representative but not independently verified | Partner declares it can provide 20 training places |
| Independent confirmation | Confirmation from a source independent of the partner's own declaration | Site visit or reference check with consent |
| Assumption | Belief used for planning but not yet tested | Expected participant demand |
| Open question | Material information not yet known | Who is the data controller for this processing? |
| Decision | An explicit choice made by a named accountable person | Pilot limited to one service location |

Do not promote a partner declaration into a verified fact without evidence. Record source, owner, date checked, expiry/recheck date and reviewer for every material claim.

### 4.1 Suitability boundary

The first pilot should be a single non-cash service or capability coordination use case. Examples for discussion only include distributing a defined quantity of agricultural inputs, arranging a specific skills-training service, or coordinating a clearly scoped water/sanitation maintenance task. None of these examples is a selected partner or approved pilot.

Do not select a pilot merely because it is easy to digitize. Select one where an accountable operator can safely perform the real-world work and evidence its completion.

### 6.1 Mandatory privacy questions

- What is the specific purpose of each data category?
- Who determines the purposes and means of processing, and in what capacity?
- What processing basis has a qualified reviewer determined for each purpose?
- What information is given to participants, when and by whom?
- Is consent relied upon or separately required? If yes, what specific choice is being requested and how can it be withdrawn?
- Which partner staff can access which fields, and why?
- Can the originating partner keep direct identity data while Atlas stores only a pseudonymous reference?
- How can a participant request access, correction, objection, withdrawal or erasure as applicable?
- How will linked evidence, exports, backups and subject-reference mappings be handled on retention expiry?
- Is an impact assessment or regulator/other consultation required before the proposed processing?
- What incident notification and response obligations apply?

A pseudonymous reference remains potentially linkable data. It must not be treated as anonymous merely because a name is absent.

The qualified safeguarding owner must determine procedures for the actual participant group and context. Do not assume a guardian, family member or partner representative is automatically authorized to consent or receive information.

No live pilot involving safeguarding-sensitive information may begin without trained human response capacity and safe referral arrangements. A platform report form is not a safeguarding service.

Participants must be able to understand what the service is, what the platform does, what the partner does, what information is shared, how to refuse optional participation and how to challenge an inaccurate record without retaliation or loss of unrelated entitlements.

## 8. Partner authority and operational controls

Before any partner-facing account access is designed, agree:

- How the organization authorizes an individual representative.
- Which account is bound to that grant.
- Which actions and specific interventions/resources are in scope.
- Who issues and reviews the grant.
- Effective date, expiry and immediate revocation procedure.
- Whether sub-delegation is prohibited or narrowly allowed.
- How authority changes are communicated and audited.
- How conflicting interests are declared and handled.
- Who can approve a match, commitment and delivery review.
- How access is removed when a staff member leaves the organization.

No authority may be inferred from an email address, an account display name, merchant status or a user-supplied actor identifier.

## 9. Evidence and outcome measurement plan

Define the operational event and evidence stages separately:

| Stage | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Need submitted | A request/opportunity was recorded | Validity, eligibility or entitlement |
| Match proposed | A capability appears feasible under disclosed criteria | Acceptance or allocation |
| Commitment accepted | An accountable contributor agreed to provide something | Delivery |
| Delivery declared | An actor says the contribution/service was delivered | Independent verification |
| Delivery reviewed | A reviewer assessed the available evidence within a stated scope | Causation or long-term impact |
| Outcome observed | A change was observed over a defined period | That the intervention alone caused the change |

### 10.1 Automatic stop conditions

Record **Blocked / Not ready** if any of the following applies:

- No accountable operator accepts responsibility for the real-world service.
- The partner cannot evidence authority to participate or appoint representatives.
- There is no trained safeguarding lead or safe escalation capacity where relevant.
- Participants cannot safely refuse, withdraw from optional participation or challenge errors.
- Data purposes, roles, processing basis, notice or retention remain materially unresolved.
- The proposed workflow requires public beneficiary discovery, individual deservingness ranking or autonomous allocation.
- The partner cannot deliver the service at the proposed capacity.
- The pilot depends on money movement or financial custody not separately authorized.
- Required CI or M028/M029 approvals are absent.
